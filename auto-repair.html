Founding pilot

Low risk by design.

  1. A 15-minute process review

    We map where your enquiries enter, who is alerted, and what happens when the first reply is missed. You receive a one-page comparison of your public enquiry path against local peers, yours to keep whether or not we go further.

  2. A written one-page scope

    What will be set up, what it will not do, and what "working" means for your business, agreed in writing before any work starts.

  3. Setup waived for founding pilots

    The ongoing fee is agreed in writing before work starts, typically between US$100 and US$250 a month depending on the size of the business and the volume of enquiries. If the agreed minimum cannot be delivered, the first payment is refunded in full.

  4. Remote delivery in your hours

    Calls, setup and support are scheduled in your business hours, by phone or video. You never have to adapt to ours.
