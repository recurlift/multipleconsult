Pricing

Three levels of personalisation for auto repair shops.

Most businesses start at level one. The review tells you which level fits; the quote puts the setup fee and the monthly fee in writing.

Level 1

Ready workflow

The core enquiry workflow, configured and tested on the channels agreed in your scope for auto repair shops.

  • Quote and booking alerts with vehicle and issue captured, your branding.
Level 2

Tailored to your process

The same workflow shaped around how your team actually handles requests.

  • Service-writer routing, bay-time offers, high-value repair flagging.
Level 3

Bespoke build

A custom application for a business with its own way of working.

  • Shop-management system integration, service reminders, advisor-level reporting.
How pricing works. Every engagement has a one-time setup fee and a recurring monthly maintenance fee. The setup fee depends on the level of personalisation: a ready workflow costs least, a bespoke build most. The monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring, changes and support. Both figures are agreed in writing after the 15-minute review and before any work starts. If the agreed minimum cannot be delivered, the setup fee is refunded.

How an engagement runs

Review, scope, setup, live.

  1. A 15-minute process review

    We map where your enquiries enter, who is alerted, and what happens when the first reply is missed. You receive a one-page comparison of your public enquiry path against local peers, yours to keep whether or not we go further.

  2. A written one-page scope and quote

    The level of personalisation, what will be set up, what it will not do, what "working" means for your business, the setup fee and the monthly fee. Agreed in writing before any work starts.

  3. Setup, then a live pilot

    Setup is completed and tested with your team over screen share. The workflow runs live for an agreed period while we measure response times against your starting point.

  4. Remote delivery in your hours

    Calls, setup and support are scheduled in your business hours, by phone or video. You never have to adapt to ours.
