What the template supports today, and what we scope

Honest about what exists.

The template can be configured for

  • A branded sales page and members area.
  • A success path built from your own step-by-step videos.
  • A home for your digital products and additional tools.
  • A community area for member discussion.
  • An assistant connected to approved material such as your videos and podcast episodes.
  • Your name, media, colours, copy and content throughout.

What we define, build and test with you

  • Confirming audience demand and choosing the format: programme, community, coaching, mentorship or hybrid.
  • Which of your sources the assistant may use, and how its answers are checked.
  • Brand design, sales copy, pricing, checkout, access rules, cancellation and refunds.
  • Enquiry forms for sponsorship, speaking, coaching and programmes, and their routing rules.
  • Calendar, email, messaging, payment and analytics connections.
  • Member onboarding, moderation, support boundaries and content updates.

What we will not say: that the assistant is you, that it carries your authority on every answer, that the app earns money while you sleep, or that it is live before it has been built and tested with you. Recording, teaching and supporting your members remains real work, and it remains yours.

Pricing

Three levels of personalisation for creators.

Most creators start with the front office or a single programme. The review tells you which level fits; the quote puts the setup fee and the monthly fee in writing.

Level 1

Ready workflow

The core enquiry workflow, configured and tested on the channels agreed in your scope for creators.

  • Opportunity inbox for your enquiry forms and messages, your branding, routing to you or one teammate.
Level 2

Tailored to your process

The same workflow shaped around how your team actually handles requests.

  • Reviewed reply drafts from your approved offers and voice, success path for one programme, community space, calendar and payment hand-off.
Level 3

Bespoke build

A custom application for a business with its own way of working.

  • Approved-content assistant with source controls, multiple programmes and tiers, member tools, reporting and integrations.
How pricing works. Every engagement has a one-time setup fee and a recurring monthly maintenance fee. The setup fee depends on the level of personalisation: a ready workflow costs least, a bespoke build most. The monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring, changes and support. Both figures are agreed in writing after the 15-minute review and before any work starts. If the agreed minimum cannot be delivered, the setup fee is refunded.

How an engagement runs

Review, scope, setup, live.

  1. A 15-minute process review

    We map where your enquiries enter, who is alerted, and what happens when the first reply is missed. You receive a one-page comparison of your public enquiry path against local peers, yours to keep whether or not we go further.

  2. A written one-page scope and quote

    The level of personalisation, what will be set up, what it will not do, what "working" means for your business, the setup fee and the monthly fee. Agreed in writing before any work starts.

  3. Setup, then a live pilot

    Setup is completed and tested with your team over screen share. The workflow runs live for an agreed period while we measure response times against your starting point.

  4. Remote delivery in your hours

    Calls, setup and support are scheduled in your business hours, by phone or video. You never have to adapt to ours.
