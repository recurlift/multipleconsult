The problem

Good companies lose jobs in the minutes after the form is submitted.

Not because the work is weak, but because nobody owns the first response. The patterns we see on contractors' own websites:

  • The site promises a reply "within one business day", while competitors on the same search page offer live chat or a same-hour call.
  • Requests land in a shared info@ inbox that is checked when someone gets back to the office.
  • Lead marketplaces send the same homeowner to several contractors at once. The first to call back usually gets the inspection.
  • Nobody can say, with a number, how long a web enquiry waited last month.

How it works

Four steps between the form and the first call.

The workflow sits in front of whatever you already use for scheduling and invoicing. It does not replace your tools, your people or your judgement.

1

Alert

The moment a request arrives, the right person is told on their phone, with the homeowner's details and a one-tap callback.

2

Qualify

The request is sorted before anyone calls: repair or replacement, insurance claim or not, urgent or planned, in your service area or not.

3

Respond

The homeowner gets an honest acknowledgement with a real time. The callback has a named owner and a reminder if it is missed.

4

Track

Every request is logged with who responded and when, so response time becomes a number you can see rather than an inbox you check.

Built around how a small crew actually works

Owners and estimators are on roofs and in crawl spaces during the hours enquiries arrive. The workflow assumes that. It alerts whoever is free, lets the first person to claim the request take it, and escalates if nobody has acted within the window you choose.

Email, text, WhatsApp or app notification, your choice. Setup and training happen over a screen share, in your business hours.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you sell leads or run advertising?

No. We only work on what happens after an enquiry you already receive arrives. If your problem is volume rather than response, we are the wrong call and will say so in the review.

Do I have to change my CRM or scheduling tool?

No. The workflow sits in front of your existing tools and hands off to them. Where you already have a system that alerts and routes well, the review will be short.

What does it cost?

Setup is waived for founding pilots. If you keep the live workflow afterwards, the proposed fee is US$250 per month, agreed in writing before any work starts, with a full refund of the first payment if the agreed minimum cannot be delivered.

Where are you based, and does that matter?

Multiple Consult operates from Kochi, India and works remotely with companies in the United States and the United Kingdom. All calls, setup and support happen in your business hours over phone or video. You are told this in the first email, not after.

Can you promise more jobs?

No. The only numbers that matter are your own: enquiry volume, time to first response, and how many enquiries turn into inspections, measured during the pilot.

How did you find my company?

By reading your website and public listings, one company at a time. Nothing automated, no purchased list. If you would rather not hear from us, reply "no" to the email and you will not be contacted again.
