The problem

Good businesses lose customers in the minutes after the message is sent.

Not because the work is weak, but because nobody owns the first reply. The patterns we see on small-business websites and profiles:

  • The site promises a reply "within one business day" while a competitor on the same search page offers live chat or a same-hour call.
  • Enquiries land in a shared inbox, or across three messaging apps, and are answered in a batch when someone is free.
  • Marketplaces and directories send the same customer to several businesses at once. The first to answer usually wins.
  • Nobody can say, with a number, how long a new enquiry waited last month, or how many never got an answer.

How it works

Four steps between the enquiry and the first reply.

The workflow sits in front of whatever you already use for booking, scheduling or invoicing. It does not replace your tools, your people or your judgement.

1

Alert

The moment a request arrives, from any channel, the right person is told on their phone with the customer's details and a one-tap reply.

2

Qualify

The request is sorted before anyone replies: urgent or planned, high-value or routine, new customer or existing, in your area or not.

3

Respond

The customer gets an honest acknowledgement with a real time. The reply has a named owner and a reminder if it is missed.

4

Track

Every enquiry is logged with who replied and when, so response time becomes a number you can see rather than an inbox you check.

Built around how a small team actually works

Owners and staff are on roofs, in chairs, behind counters and under cars during the hours enquiries arrive. The workflow assumes that. It alerts whoever is free, lets the first person to claim the request take it, and escalates if nobody has acted within the window you choose.

Email, text, WhatsApp or app notification, your choice. Setup and training happen over a screen share, in your business hours.

Pricing

Three levels of personalisation. One clear quote.

Most businesses start at level one and move up only when their own enquiry numbers justify it. The review tells you which level fits, and the quote puts both fees in writing.

Level 1

Ready workflow

The proven enquiry workflow, set up on your channels. Live in days.

  • Alerts for your existing forms and messages, your branding, your team on their phones.
Level 2

Tailored to your process

The same workflow shaped around how your team actually handles requests.

  • Custom qualifying questions, routing rules by urgency, area or team, hand-off to your booking or CRM tool.
Level 3

Bespoke build

A custom application for a business with its own way of working.

  • Customer-facing pages, multi-location dispatch, integrations and reporting built for your operation.
How pricing works. Every engagement has a one-time setup fee and a recurring monthly maintenance fee. The setup fee depends on the level of personalisation: a ready workflow costs least, a bespoke build most. The monthly fee covers hosting, monitoring, changes and support. Both figures are agreed in writing after the 15-minute review and before any work starts. If the agreed minimum cannot be delivered, the setup fee is refunded.

How an engagement runs

Review, scope, setup, live.

  1. A 15-minute process review

    We map where your enquiries enter, who is alerted, and what happens when the first reply is missed. You receive a one-page comparison of your public enquiry path against local peers, yours to keep whether or not we go further.

  2. A written one-page scope and quote

    The level of personalisation, what will be set up, what it will not do, what "working" means for your business, the setup fee and the monthly fee. Agreed in writing before any work starts.

  3. Setup, then a live pilot

    Setup is completed and tested with your team over screen share. The workflow runs live for an agreed period while we measure response times against your starting point.

  4. Remote delivery in your hours

    Calls, setup and support are scheduled in your business hours, by phone or video. You never have to adapt to ours.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you sell leads or run advertising?

No. We only work on what happens after an enquiry you already receive arrives. If your problem is volume rather than response, we are the wrong call and will say so in the review.

Do I have to change my booking or CRM tool?

No. The workflow sits in front of your existing tools and hands off to them. Where you already have a system that alerts and routes well, the review will be short.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee plus a recurring monthly maintenance fee. The setup fee depends on how far the workflow is personalised to your business, from a ready workflow to a bespoke build. Both figures are agreed in writing after the review and before any work starts, and the setup fee is refunded if the agreed minimum cannot be delivered.

Where are you based, and does that matter?

Multiple Consult operates from Kochi, India and works remotely with businesses in the United States and the United Kingdom. All calls, setup and support happen in your business hours over phone or video. You are told this in the first email, not after.

Can you promise more customers?

No. The only numbers that matter are your own: enquiry volume, time to first reply, and how many enquiries turn into appointments or jobs, measured during the pilot.

How did you find my business?

By reading your website and public profiles, one business at a time. Nothing automated, no purchased list. If you would rather not hear from us, reply "no" to the email and you will not be contacted again.
