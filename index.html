Answer the enquiry before the homeowner books someone else.

Multiple Consult works with independent roofing, HVAC, plumbing, remodelling and other home-service companies on one narrow problem: a quote request arrives through the website or a lead marketplace while the owner or estimator is on a job, and nobody owns the first call.

Request a 15-minute process review

What a lead-response workflow does

  1. Alert The moment a request arrives, the right person is told on their phone, with the homeowner's details and a one-tap callback.
  2. Qualify The request is sorted before the first call: repair or replacement, insurance claim or not, urgent or planned.
  3. Respond The homeowner gets an honest acknowledgement with a real time, and the callback has a named owner and a reminder if it is missed.
  4. Track Every request is logged with who responded and when, so response time becomes a number you can see rather than an inbox you check.

We do not sell leads, run advertising, or replace your existing scheduling or CRM tools. The workflow sits in front of them.

How a founding pilot works

  • A 15-minute review. We map where your enquiries enter, who is alerted, and what happens when the first call is missed. You receive a one-page comparison of your public enquiry path against local peers, whether or not we go further.
  • A written one-page scope. What will be built, what it will not do, and what "working" means for your business, agreed before any work starts.
  • Setup waived for founding pilots. If you keep the live workflow after the pilot, the proposed fee is US$250 per month. If the agreed minimum cannot be delivered, the first payment is refunded.
  • Remote delivery in your hours. Calls, setup and support are scheduled in your business hours, by phone or video.

No results or revenue are promised. Your own enquiry volume, response time and close rate, measured during the pilot, are the only numbers that matter.
